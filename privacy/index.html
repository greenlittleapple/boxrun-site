Privacy

Updated 28 October 2026.

Your saves stay on your PC

Your routes, times, ghosts and settings are saved on your PC, in the storage that Steam's in-game browser keeps for save.boxrun.gg. BoxRun opens that small page inside the game to save and load. Your saves aren't sent anywhere.

What SHARE publishes

Pressing SHARE publishes that route and its ghost to BoxRun's server, api.boxrun.gg, and gives it a short code. Anyone with the code can load the route and race the ghost. Loading a code, or checking a loaded route for a newer version, sends a request for that code. BR1 codes, the long kind, work without the server.

What the server keeps

  • The routes and ghosts people publish.
  • A scrambled form (a keyed hash) of the internet address that sent a request, never the address itself. It's used to limit how often one player can publish, to block spam, to handle reports, and to count each player who loads a code once.
  • The hourly counters for those limits expire within a day or two. Reports are kept until the route is reviewed.
  • Server logs hold no request contents and are deleted after 14 days.

The server never sees your Steam account, your saves or your best times, and sets no cookies. Scrambled isn't the same as anonymous: whoever holds the server's key could match a hash to an address, and only the server and Marcus hold it.

Visits to boxrun.gg

The home page and the press page count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies. This page and the save page don't count visits.

Nothing else

Nothing else is collected. Questions, or a route of yours you want taken down: ask in the BoxRun Discord.
