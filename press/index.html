BoxRun

BoxRun press kit

BoxRun is a free mod for Deadlock that adds a route editor to the game. Players place checkpoints anywhere on the Midtown map, run the route against a clock and an animated ghost of the best run, and share it as a short code (like BR-882EMZ) that anyone can import and race. Routes can mark boxes that must be broken, carry notes, split into legs, and start with the Soul Urn. BoxRun is its own map that the player loads, so normal matches aren't touched. It was released on GameBanana on 28 October 2026 by Marcus (greenlittleapple).

What BoxRun uses from the game

BoxRun is a fan-made mod for Deadlock and isn't made by or affiliated with Valve. It doesn't include Valve's map. When you load BoxRun, it loads Midtown from your own Deadlock install, so the world is always the version you have. The hero portraits, rank badges, icons, fonts and sounds in BoxRun's menus also come from your installed game, so no copies of them are in the download.

The download holds BoxRun's own files: its menus and scripts, effects, sample routes, and signs and numbers drawn as pictures in Deadlock's fonts. It also includes edited settings for Midtown's boxes and health pickups, and a small minimap file so the map shows on BoxRun's own map.

The pictures for shared route links are made on BoxRun's server using Deadlock's fonts, rank badges and the Midtown minimap. The font files stay on that server and are never offered for download. Screenshots and videos here are from the game. BoxRun is free, with nothing to buy.
