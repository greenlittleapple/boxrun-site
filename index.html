Speedrun and challenge routes for Deadlock

BoxRun is a free mod with a route editor in the game. A route is a set of checkpoints anywhere on the map, run against a clock and an animated ghost, and shared as one short code that friends import and race.

How it works

  1. Making a route

    Shoot NEW ROUTE in the BoxRun menu, walk to each spot and shoot PLACE. The first point is the start and the last is the finish. In TEST, run it, then use that run as the time to beat, which fills in the three tiers, and as the route's ghost.

  2. Sharing a route

    SHARE copies one short code that carries the route and its ghost. If the online library can't be reached, it copies the full code instead, which works anywhere.

    A sample route's code, HK Yellow 1 (open):

    BR-882EMZ

  3. Racing a route

    Your friends shoot IMPORT A CODE, paste it and go to the start. Their run starts as they leave the start gate, with your ghost beside them. The cards over the start and finish show the tiers, their best, their splits, and the hero and souls each time was set with.

Kinds of routes

Speedruns

A loop through the map with three tier times to reach.

Box runs

Mark the boxes a run has to break. Every box is back at the start of each run.

Movement challenges

Wall jumps, dash jumps and zipline lines, timed from checkpoint to checkpoint.

Rotations

A timed trip from one spot to another that you often make in matches.

Soul Urn runs

The urn starts on the ground at a spot you pick, or carried from the start.

What a route holds

  • Guided or open. A guided route has a checkpoint at every turn and notes that explain the line. An open route has a start, a finish and the boxes to break, in any order and by any path.
  • Boxes to break. A run that misses one shows its time as Incomplete, with the box it missed, and doesn't count as a best.
  • Three tiers. Eternus, Oracle and Initiate times. The finish card shows the tier you reached and how far the next one is.
  • Splits and legs. Your time at every checkpoint against your best, named legs, and your best possible time from your fastest sections.
  • Notes. Up to 50 tips placed on the route, each shown near the checkpoint it belongs to.
  • The Soul Urn. On the ground at a spot you choose, or carried from the start.

Ghosts

Every run is recorded. Your best comes back as an animated, see-through copy of the hero you ran it with, and it leaves the start gate when you do.

A route can also carry its maker's ghost. You race that one until your best beats it, then yours, and you can switch between them on the route's page. A short code brings the route's ghost along, so a friend who imports your route races your run. Ghosts can be turned off in PRACTICE.

The map

BoxRun loads the Deadlock map from the game files on your PC when it starts and sets it up for practice.

  • One team. Every route plays on one team, so every zipline rides end to end and nothing on the map attacks you.
  • Resets. Every box comes back, dropped souls and power-ups are cleared and cooldowns reset when a run starts, when a route loads and when you start editing.
  • PRACTICE tools. Max souls, noclip, infinite ammo, game speed, camps on or off, the Soul Urn, and RESET THE MAP or RELOAD THE MAP when something's off. Runs below 100% game speed don't count as bests.

    Questions

    How do I install it?

    With a mod manager like Grimoire, install it from the GameBanana page and start Deadlock from the manager. Then open the console and type map boxrun_midtown. Give it up to a minute after you spawn in, while the mod loads and the BoxRun menu appears in front of you. It can take longer on some PCs, so wait for the menu. The GameBanana page also has steps for installing by hand.

    If you close the menu, press 0 to bring it back (with hotkeys on: BoxRun asks at your first start, and HELP > HOTKEYS turns them on later), or type boxrun in the console. Both bring the menu in front of you.

    Optional, no console needed: also install MapList after BoxRun. Then press Esc in the hideout: BoxRun is under CUSTOM MAPS, at the bottom of the friends column on the right. Click it to load.

    What does it change on my PC?

    BoxRun is one 42 MB addon file holding a small map you load yourself and play alone. That map loads the Deadlock map from the game files on your PC instead of carrying a copy, and it never changes them. There's no DLL, no injection and no replaced game file, and nothing changes in normal matches. A mod manager adds the addons lines to Deadlock's gameinfo.gi file, as it does for every mod.

    Do I need to keep anything running beside the game?

    No. Everything happens in the in-game menu.

    Where are my routes saved?

    On your PC, in the storage of the game's built-in browser, through a small BoxRun page at save.boxrun.gg. Saving needs the internet. Offline, new routes and times last until you quit, and full route codes still work.

    Does BoxRun send anything online?

    Only when you use a short code. Publishing a route, or looking one up by its BR- code, sends that request through BoxRun's save page to api.boxrun.gg. The server keeps the routes and ghosts people publish, plus a scrambled form of the internet address that sent them, so spam can be blocked. It never sees your saves, your Steam account or your best times. Full BR1 codes work without it.

    Can I race my friends live?

    Not yet. Deadlock doesn't let players join custom servers the usual way, and live races are being tested with friends. For now you race each other's times and ghosts by sharing route codes.

    Are there leaderboards?

    Not yet. Each route has three tier times, your best, your splits and a ghost. An online library of community routes, with leaderboards, is on the list for after launch.

    Does the hero matter?

    Any hero works. Each time is stored with the hero and whether max souls were on, and a ghost is always the hero who ran it.

    Something on the map is stuck or missing. What do I do?

    In the menu, PRACTICE has RESET THE MAP, which brings back the boxes, the urn and the ghost and puts you at the route's start, and RELOAD THE MAP, which loads the map again with your routes kept. boxrun_reset in the console does the same as RESET THE MAP.

    What happens when the map changes in a patch?

    BoxRun uses the Deadlock map from your installed game, so the world follows Valve's map patches with no BoxRun download. If a patch moves boxes or paths, routes made before it may need adjusting, and any BoxRun update that follows goes up on the GameBanana page.

    Where do I report a bug or share a route?

    In the BoxRun Discord. Post route codes in the routes forum, and say what broke and what you were doing when it happened.

    How do I remove it?

    Remove it in your mod manager, or delete the addon file the install notes name. To clear your saved routes first, type boxrun_clear_saves in the console while on the map.
