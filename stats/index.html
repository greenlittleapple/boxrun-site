Usage

Short codes and menu opens from api.boxrun.gg, and the GameBanana page. Times are Pacific. Our own PC and test routes are left out.

Stats key

Paste the stats key. It's in work/boxrun-stats/stats-key.txt in the BoxRun project. This browser keeps it.

Hover a chart (or tap and hold) to read every chart at that time. Click a chart to open it full width; Esc closes it. Players are counted by scrambled internet address, so a household can count once and a player on two networks twice.

Funnel

From games with counts on (2.1 and later), new installs only. A 2.0.x player who updated to 2.1 isn't a new install: it sends no first-time steps and no returns, though its days and runs count in the weekly numbers. An install's first day is the Pacific day of its first map load.

The funnel appears when the server sends it.
First dayMap loadsMenuStarted a runFinished a run Counted finishBack next dayBack days 7-13

Each row is the installs whose first day it was: how many reached each step at least once (on any day; a step on a later day is filed under the first day) and how many came back the next day or on any of days 7 to 13. Shares are of that row's map loads. Pending means the window for coming back isn't over yet, so the count can still grow. The counts are a floor: games with counts off, offline or older than 2.1 send nothing.

Routes

Every route shared as a short code. A route's players count each player once per route, not counting its maker, so the column doesn't add up to the Players since launch tile. Our PC isn't counted, so a sample can show one fewer than "Raced by N" in game. Held routes are marked red. Alias codes (admin.py alias add) are listed under their route's code with their label and the players who loaded the route through them; those players are in the route's count too.

The route list appears when the server sends it.

A dash means that day's counts expired before they were saved. Menu opened counts the players whose game asked for the route of the week (once a session, at the first menu: online, BoxRun 2.0.6 and later). Back from the day before is how many of them also opened it the day before.

Link clicks per day

No /go links yet (admin.py go add).

Clicks on api.boxrun.gg/go/<name> per Pacific day, a plain count: link previewers, our PC and more than 30 an hour from one address aren't counted. Nothing about who clicked is kept.

Discord

Discord doesn't share join numbers without a bot. Joins per invite are under the server's Server Settings > Invites, and member growth under Server Insights. A bot that reads them would need setting up from Marcus's Discord account, so it's left for later.
