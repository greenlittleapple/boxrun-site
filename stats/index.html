How it's counted
  • Times are Pacific. Our own PC and test routes are left out.
  • Players by address (addresses that loaded a code, menu players, back from the day before) are counted by scrambled internet address, so a household can count once and a player on two networks twice. Menu players: every online game asks for the route of the week once a session, at its first menu (BoxRun 2.0.6 and later).
  • Installs (players today, the funnel, weekly actives, runs) come from games with counts on, 2.1 and later. New installs only in the funnel: a 2.0.x player who updated sends no first-time steps or returns, though its days and runs count in the weekly numbers. An install's first day is the Pacific day of its first map load.
  • Funnel steps: at least once, any day, filed under the first day. Floor: counts off, offline and pre-2.1 games send nothing. Menu: queued, not seen. A lost answer can count a step twice (under 1%).
  • The main bars use first days 2 to 8 days ago, so every next-day window has closed. Week-1 return (back on days 7 to 13) uses first days 14 to 20 days ago. Each row's change is against the 7 first days before. The biggest loss is marked within the first session (first load to counted finish).
  • Downloads → first load: first loads since 2.1 over GameBanana downloads since 2.1 (the total less 3,800 from files removed before 2.1). A lower bound on installs: update downloads inflate the downloads, and games with counts off or offline send no first load.
  • 2.3 installs: the steps between the first load and the first run, sent only by installs first loaded on 2.3 (counted from Nov 10), as shares of the 2.3 map loads. Shot at it needs no Menu seen and READY no GO TO START (a walk-in gate start), so there's no loss line into them.
  • This week against last week: this week's complete days (Monday so far on a Monday) against last week's same days. Weekly actives add up each install's first play day of the week, so they're distinct installs.
