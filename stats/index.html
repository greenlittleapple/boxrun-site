BoxRun

Usage

Short codes from api.boxrun.gg and the GameBanana page. Days are Pacific time. Our own PC and test routes are left out.

Stats key

Paste the stats key. It's in work/boxrun-stats/stats-key.txt in the Deadlock project. This browser keeps it.

Short codes per day

DayCodes sharedNew versionsGhosts uploadedCode lookups Players who loaded a codePlayers who sharedReports

A lookup is an import, an update check or a ghost download. Players are counted by scrambled internet address, so a household can count once and a player on two networks twice. A dash means that day's lookup counts expired before they were saved.

Discord

Discord doesn't share join numbers without a bot. Joins per invite are under the server's Server Settings > Invites, and member growth under Server Insights. A bot that reads them would need setting up from Marcus's Discord account, so it's left for later.
